About

Hello! I am a second-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Stanford University, where I am advised by Prof. Kawin Setsompop and Prof. John M. Pauly.

My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning, computer vision, and biomedical imaging. Specifically, I focus on optimizing data acquisition and image reconstruction using deep learning-based accelerated MRI techniques. My goal is to develop next-generation medical imaging methods that significantly contribute to patient diagnosis and care.
